## 1. Conventions (all regimes)

| # | Convention |
|---|---|
| 1.1 | Purchase on 1 January of year 1. Sale on 31 December of year H. Holding period = **H full years** (for the allowance schedule, §3.3). |
| 1.2 | Tax year y = simulation year y. The **2026 rates and thresholds are frozen** for the whole horizon. They are not indexed, and later laws are not anticipated. |
| 1.3 | IR on rental income = **base × TMI** (marginal rate on the whole base, no bracket crossing, no décote or quotient). PS = base × the PS rate of the income category (§2). |
| 1.4 | Deductibility of CSG (6.8 points deductible from the next year's taxable income) is **ignored**. This is a known simplification, and it slightly overstates tax. |
| 1.5 | One tax household (foyer), one seller (cédant) per property. The surtax threshold applies per cédant (§3.5, [CONFIRM 11.9]). |
| 1.6 | Loan: monthly French annuity. Insurance = initial principal × rate / 12 each month (same as `financial.py`, which is correct). The loan interest and loan insurance of a year are those of its 12 months. |
| 1.7 | Market value at the end of year H = price × (1 + appreciation)^H. |
| 1.8 | Rent collected in year y = monthly rent × 12 × (1 + rent indexation)^(y−1) × (1 − vacancy). Management = % of collected rent. Fixed charges (co-ownership charges, TF, accounting fees, CFE) are indexed by (1 + charges indexation)^(y−1). |
| 1.9 | Works (`budget_travaux`, `renovation_energetique`) and furniture are paid at t0 and financed by apport + loan. They are not a cash outflow in year 1. Their **tax** treatment depends on the regime (§4). |
| 1.10 | Plus-values are computed **per property** and floored at 0 per property. There is no netting across properties (CGI 150 U: each sale is taxed on its own). |
| 1.11 | Rounding: compute in floats. Golden values are rounded to the cent; compare with a 1 € tolerance (the app's loan schedule rounds each month to the cent). |

## 3. Plus-value of individuals (LMNP réel, micro-BIC, micro-foncier, foncier réel)

Inputs per property: `price`, `notary_fees`, `works` (+ `energy_works`), `sale_price`, `sale_fees_pct`, `H`, `regime`, `residence_services: bool`, and from the yearly state `cum_amort_deducted_imm` (LMNP réel only).

```
prix_cession   = sale_price − sale_fees                          # §3.1
acq_fees       = max(notary_fees, 7.5% × price)                  # §3.2
eligible_works = 0 if regime == foncier_reel else works          # §3.2, works already deducted are excluded
works_maj      = max(eligible_works, 15% × price) if H > 5 else eligible_works
addback        = cum_amort_deducted_imm if (regime == lmnp_reel and not residence_services) else 0   # §3.4
prix_acq       = price + acq_fees + works_maj − addback
PV             = max(0, prix_cession − prix_acq)                 # per property
base_IR        = PV × (1 − abatt_IR(H));   IR    = 19% × base_IR
base_PS        = PV × (1 − abatt_PS(H));   PS    = 17.2% × base_PS
surtax         = surtax(base_IR)                                  # §3.5
exit_tax       = IR + PS + surtax (+ déficit-foncier recapture, §4.5)
```

### 3.1 Sale costs
Costs borne by the seller (agency fee when it is charged to the seller, diagnostics) reduce the sale price (CGI 150 VA). The model treats `frais_vente_pct` as such costs.

### 3.2 Acquisition price increases (CGI 150 VB II; BOI-RFPI-PVI-20-10-20-20)
- Acquisition fees: the actual amount, or a **7.5 % flat rate** on the price. The model takes the larger.
- Works: actual construction or improvement works, **only if they were not already taken into account for IR** (deducted from revenus fonciers, or giving a tax reduction). Alternatively, a **15 % flat rate** on the price when the property is sold **more than 5 years** after purchase. With the flat rate, it does not matter whether the works were deducted (§350 of that BOI). The model takes the larger.
- The flat rates are computed on the contractual price (`price`), not on the price reduced by the add-back [CONFIRM 11.5].
- LMNP réel: the real works are amortized and their amortization is added back (§3.4). The spec therefore treats them as eligible [CONFIRM 11.4]. All golden cases have works ≤ 15 % of the price and H > 5, so the golden values do not depend on this point.

### 3.3 Holding-period allowances (CGI 150 VC; BOI-RFPI-PVI-20-20)
```
abatt_IR(H) = 0 if H ≤ 5;  6% × (H − 5) for 6 ≤ H ≤ 21 (96% at 21);  100% if H ≥ 22
abatt_PS(H) = 0 if H ≤ 5;  1.65% × (H − 5) for 6 ≤ H ≤ 21 (26.4% at 21);  28% at 22;
              28% + 9% × (H − 22) for 23 ≤ H ≤ 29;  100% if H ≥ 30
```
Check values: H = 10 → 30 % / 8.25 %; 15 → 60 % / 16.5 %; 17 → 72 % / 19.8 %; 20 → 90 % / 24.75 %; 21 → 96 % / 26.4 %; 22 → 100 % / 28 %; 25 → 100 % / 55 %; 30 → 100 % / 100 %.

### 3.4 LMNP amortization add-back (CGI 150 VB III, LF 2025 = loi 2025-127 of 14 Feb 2025, art. 84)
- Applies to sales from **15 February 2025** (impots.gouv; one law firm says 16 February). Every sale the model simulates is later than that, so the add-back always applies.
- The acquisition price is reduced by the amortization **actually deducted** (39 C), on the building components only. Furniture amortization is not added back (furniture is not part of the real-estate gain), and neither is the ARD stock still carried forward at the sale.
- The building components are: building including its share of notary fees, plus works (§4.1). So `cum_amort_deducted_imm` covers all non-furniture amortization deducted (§4.2 explains how it is tracked).
- **Exception: residences-services.** Student residences, senior residences, and residences for elderly or disabled people (impots.gouv; the law firm refers to CCH L631-12) are not affected. Per-archetype flag `residence_services: bool = False`.
- Micro-BIC: no amortization was deducted, so there is no add-back.

### 3.5 Surtax on high plus-values (CGI 1609 nonies G; BOI-RFPI-TPVIE-20)
The base is the IR-taxable gain **after** the IR allowance (`base_IR`), per sale and per cédant. It applies when `base_IR > 50,000`, at a rate that applies from the first euro:

| base_IR (PV) | surtax |
|---|---|
| 50,001 – 60,000 | 2 % × PV − (60,000 − PV) × 1/20 |
| 60,001 – 100,000 | 2 % × PV |
| 100,001 – 110,000 | 3 % × PV − (110,000 − PV) × 1/10 |
| 110,001 – 150,000 | 3 % × PV |
| 150,001 – 160,000 | 4 % × PV − (160,000 − PV) × 15/100 |
| 160,001 – 200,000 | 4 % × PV |
| 200,001 – 210,000 | 5 % × PV − (210,000 − PV) × 20/100 |
| 210,001 – 250,000 | 5 % × PV |
| 250,001 – 260,000 | 6 % × PV − (260,000 − PV) × 25/100 |
| > 260,000 | 6 % × PV |

When a married couple sells a property they own together, the threshold applies to each spouse's share [CONFIRM 11.9].

## 4. Regimes

### 4.1 Amortizable components (LMNP réel and SCI IS only)
| Component | Base | Default duration (allowed range) | Stops |
|---|---|---|---|
| Land | `(price + notary_fees) × land%` | not amortizable | – |
| Building | `(price + notary_fees) × (1 − land%)` | 30 years (25–40) | after its duration |
| Works (`budget_travaux + renovation_energetique`) | the amount | 15 years (10–15) | after its duration |
| Furniture | `mobilier` | 10 years (5–10) | after its duration |

- Straight-line, a full year from year 1 (no pro rata temporis, since the purchase is on 1 January). Component amortization is required (CGI 39 C; BOI-BIC-AMT-20-40-10-20). The four lines above are the model's simplification of it.
- `land%` (`ratio_terrain_pct`) defaults to **15 %**, and to 20 % for Lyon and Annecy (T22). It is never 0 by default.
- **Acquisition costs (scope decision):** notary fees are **capitalized**, split pro rata between land and building, and amortized with the building. Expensing them in year 1 is a legal option (CGI ann. III art. 38 quinquies) that the model does not offer. A year-1 charge would mostly create a BIC deficit that expires after 10 years, or ARD, so capitalizing is the neutral, standard choice. See §9.
- Unfurnished regimes (micro-foncier, foncier réel) **never** amortize. T23 adds a regression test for this.

### 4.2 LMNP réel (BIC, régime réel)
Inputs per year: collected rent, operating charges (co-ownership charges, TF, management, accounting fees, CFE), loan interest + loan insurance, the component amortization of the year.

State carried from year to year: `ard_imm`, `ard_mob` (deferred amortization, no time limit), `bic_deficits: list[(year_created, amount)]` (10 years), `cum_amort_deducted_imm`, `cum_amort_deducted_mob`.

```
expire bic_deficits created before y − 10
R = rent − operating_charges − interest − loan_insurance              # result before amortization
cur_imm = building + works amortization of the year (0 once fully amortized)
cur_mob = furniture amortization of the year
pool_imm = cur_imm + ard_imm ; pool_mob = cur_mob + ard_mob ; pool = pool_imm + pool_mob
if R ≤ 0:
    bic_deficits.append((y, −R)) ; deducted = 0 ; benefice = 0
else:
    deducted = min(R, pool)        # CGI 39 C II: amortization cannot create or increase a deficit
    benefice = R − deducted
ded_imm = deducted × pool_imm / pool ; ded_mob = deducted − ded_imm   # pro-rata convention [CONFIRM 11.3]
ard_imm = pool_imm − ded_imm ; ard_mob = pool_mob − ded_mob
cum_amort_deducted_imm += ded_imm ; cum_amort_deducted_mob += ded_mob
base = benefice − use_fifo(bic_deficits, benefice)    # prior BIC deficits, oldest first
IR = base × TMI ; PS = base × 18.6%
```
- Order: amortization is limited first (39 C II limits it to "loyers − autres charges" of the same year), then prior deficits are set against what remains [CONFIRM 11.2]. The current code uses the opposite order. Every LMNP golden case has R > 0 in all years, so the golden values do not depend on this point.
- BIC non-pro deficits offset only BIC non-pro income (furnished lets), never the global income (CGI 156 I 1° ter).
- Several properties: the 39 C limit is applied **per property** (each property's amortization ≤ its own R⁺). The properties' `benefice` and deficits are then netted within the year at the foyer level, because the foyer's LMNP result is one BIC result [CONFIRM 11.10].
- CFE and accounting fees are deductible charges.
- LMP: if the portfolio's furnished receipts are > 23,000 €, show the warning "possible LMP status: social contributions and a different plus-value regime apply; not modelled". Nothing else (§9).

### 4.3 Micro-BIC
```
abatt = max(305, rent × a)     a = 50% (long-term or classified tourist letting) | 30% (non-classified tourist letting)
base = max(0, rent − abatt) ; IR = base × TMI ; PS = base × 18.6%
```
- No charges, no amortization, no deficit. CFE is still paid (it affects cash flow, not the tax base).
- Eligibility is per foyer, on the **sum** of furnished receipts across all properties: ≤ 83,600 € (2026 income) for long-term or classified letting, ≤ 15,000 € for non-classified. Above the ceiling, the micro regime is unavailable. Show a warning and force LMNP réel. (The law allows one extra year after the ceiling is first exceeded. The model ignores that tolerance.)
- Plus-value: §3 with no add-back.
- `micro_bic_abattement_pct` in the archetype or UI takes only the values 50 or 30. The old 71 % rate is gone for 2026 income.

### 4.4 Micro-foncier
```
base = rent × 70% ; IR = base × TMI ; PS = base × 17.2%
```
- Eligible when the foyer's gross foncier income (all unfurnished properties) is ≤ 15,000 €. Otherwise régime réel is mandatory. Opting for réel is irrevocable for 3 years. The model applies the chosen regime for the whole horizon.
- No deductions, no deficit, no CFE. Plus-value: §3. Works were not deducted, so real works are eligible for the majoration.

### 4.5 Revenus fonciers, régime réel (déficit foncier)
State: `foncier_deficits: list[(year_created, amount)]` (10 years, against revenus fonciers only), `global_imputed_by_year: dict[year, amount]`.

```
expire foncier_deficits created before y − 10
I = interest + loan_insurance                            # "intérêts d'emprunt" line (form 2044, line 250)
C = management + co-ownership charges + TF + other deductible charges + works paid this year
net = rent − I − C                                       # computed over ALL unfurnished properties of the foyer
if net < 0:
    D = −net
    G = min(D, C)                                        # rents absorb interest first; the interest part never goes to global income
    cap = 10,700 (+ up to 10,700 more for eligible energy works paid ≤ 2027, see below)
    imputed = min(G, cap)
    foncier_deficits.append((y, D − imputed)) ; base = 0
else:
    imputed = 0 ; base = net − use_fifo(foncier_deficits, net)
IR = base × TMI − imputed × TMI        # the imputation on global income saves IR at TMI, never PS
PS = base × 17.2%
```
- Works: `budget_travaux` is treated as deductible repair and improvement work (not reconstruction or extension), **deducted in full in year 1** (paid in year 1). They are therefore excluded from the plus-value majoration; only the 15 % flat rate applies (§3.2).
- Energy works (`renovation_energetique`) that lift the DPE from E/F/G to A–D and are paid by 31 Dec 2027: cap = 10,700 + min(10,700, energy works included in the deficit), so at most 21,400 [CONFIRM 11.6]. After 2027, cap = 10,700. Simulation year y is calendar year 2025 + y (year 1 = 2026).
- **Recapture:** a deficit imputed on global income in year N is clawed back if the letting stops before 31 Dec of N + 3. Sale at the end of year H: add back `Σ imputed(N) × TMI` for N > H − 3 to the exit tax.
- Not modelled: the 20 € flat rate for management costs per property, the 6-year carryforward when the global income is insufficient (the model assumes enough other income).
- Out of scope: the **dispositif Jeanbrun** (statut du bailleur privé, LF 2026, loi 2026-103), an amortization for new unfurnished lets with capped rents. Flagged in §11.13.

### 4.6 SCI at IS
State: `is_losses: list` (no time limit, CGI 209 I; the 1 M€ + 50 % cap is irrelevant at this scale), `cum_amort`.

```
res = rent − operating_charges − interest − loan_insurance − amortization(building + works + furniture, §4.1)
if res < 0: is_losses += −res ; base = 0
else:       base = res − use(is_losses, res)
IS = 15% × min(base, 42,500) + 25% × max(0, base − 42,500)
```
- No IR and no PS on the company's yearly result. The investor's TMI only matters for distributions.
- Amortization **may** create a loss (there is no 39 C II limit for an IS company that lets unfurnished property). Notary fees are capitalized as in §4.1.
- **Exit:** `VNC = price + notary + works + furniture − cum_amort`; `PV = sale_price − sale_fees − VNC` (professional gain: no allowance, no surtax). The gain falls in the same tax year as the year-H result: `IS_exit = IS(base_H + PV_after_remaining_losses) − IS(base_H)`. A capital loss reduces base_H (floored at 0).
- **Distribution (scope decision, §9):** `sci_distribute_at_exit = True` by default. The company keeps the yearly cash flow and pays it out at liquidation. `boni = (sale_price − sale_fees − CRD − IRA − IS_exit) + Σ CF − apport`; `PFU = 31.4 % × max(0, boni)`; `net_exit_proceeds = proceeds − PFU`. With the flag off, the result is the company-level wealth.
- The IRR keeps the yearly cash flows at their dates (§5). This is an approximation: in reality positive cash flow stays in the company and negative cash flow is funded through an associate's current account.
- CFE: only if the SCI lets furnished property (by default the SCI lets unfurnished, so no CFE).

## 5. Cash flow, exit, wealth, IRR (all regimes)

```
CF_y        = rent − operating_charges(incl. CFE) − loan debt service (P&I + insurance) − tax_y
              tax_y = IR + PS (individuals; IR may be negative in a déficit-foncier year) or IS (SCI)
apport      = price + notary + works + furniture − loan_amount
CRD_H       = loan balance after 12·H months (0 if the loan has ended)
IRA         = min(3% × CRD_H, 6 months of interest on CRD_H) if apply_ira and H < loan_years
              (Code de la consommation R313-25)
proceeds    = sale_price − sale_fees − CRD_H − IRA − exit_tax          (individuals)
            = sale_price − sale_fees − CRD_H − IRA − IS_exit − PFU     (SCI IS)
wealth      = proceeds + Σ_{y=1..H} CF_y − apport                      # T24: replaces enrichissement_net
IRR         = irr([−apport, CF_1, …, CF_{H−1}, CF_H + proceeds])        # None (not 0) if it does not converge
```
**CFE** (furnished properties, under micro-BIC or réel): 0 in year 1 (year the establishment is created, CGI 1478 II). From year 2 on, `cfe_annual` (indexed) if that year's receipts are > 5,000 €, otherwise 0 [CONFIRM 11.8]. Never charged on unfurnished property.

## 7. Engine shape for T23 (suggestion)
```python
class TaxRegime(Protocol):
    name: str
    def compute_year(self, ctx: YearContext, state: TaxState) -> TaxResult: ...
    def compute_exit(self, ctx: ExitContext, state: TaxState) -> ExitTax: ...

@dataclass
class YearContext:   # one property, one year
    year: int; rent: float; operating_charges: float; cfe: float
    interest: float; loan_insurance: float; works_paid: float; energy_works_paid: float
    amort_building: float; amort_works: float; amort_furniture: float; tmi: float

@dataclass
class TaxState:
    ard_imm: float = 0; ard_mob: float = 0
    deficits: list[tuple[int, float]] = field(default_factory=list)    # BIC or foncier, 10 years
    is_losses: float = 0
    cum_amort_deducted_imm: float = 0; cum_amort_deducted_mob: float = 0
    global_imputed_by_year: dict[int, float] = field(default_factory=dict)

@dataclass
class TaxResult:  taxable_base: float; ir: float; ps: float; is_: float; total: float
@dataclass
class ExitTax:    plus_value: float; ir: float; ps: float; surtax: float; is_: float; pfu: float; total: float; details: dict
```
Implementations: `LmnpReel`, `MicroBic(abattement)`, `MicroFoncier`, `FoncierReel`, `SciIs(distribute_at_exit)`. Foyer-level aggregation (§8) sits in a thin `TaxHousehold` wrapper that nets results by category. A single-property run (all golden cases) needs no aggregation.

## 8. Choosing the regime and aggregating across properties
- **The law applies one regime per category of income for the whole foyer, not per property.** All furnished properties are under micro-BIC, or all under réel. All unfurnished properties are under micro-foncier, or all under réel. The micro ceilings are checked on the sum of receipts. The DEV_PLAN wording for T23 ("chosen per property") must be read this way: the property's `meuble` flag picks its category, and the user picks one regime per category [CONFIRM 11.11].
  - `meuble=False` → foncier category: micro-foncier (if ≤ 15,000 €) or réel.
  - `meuble=True` → BIC category: micro-BIC (if under the ceiling) or LMNP réel.
  - The global "SCI IS" option puts every property in the SCI.
- Deficits and the 10,700 cap apply at foyer level. Plus-values are always per property (§1.10).

## 9. Scope decisions
1. **SCI IS dividends:** model them, as the PFU (31.4 %) on the boni de liquidation at exit, with the flag `sci_distribute_at_exit` (default True). Without it, SCI IS looks much better than direct ownership: in C7 the PFU is 54,666 € against a wealth of 119,430 €. The barème option (40 % allowance) and yearly distributions are not modelled.
2. **LMP:** flag only (§4.2). No URSSAF contributions, no professional plus-value regime.
3. **Acquisition costs (LMNP réel and SCI IS):** capitalized and amortized with the building, land share excluded (§4.1). At exit, the individuals' gain uses max(real fees, 7.5 %) (§3.2), and the amortization of the fees is part of the add-back.
4. **Simplifications** that stay: marginal-rate IR, CSG deductibility ignored, thresholds frozen at 2026, no LMP, no Jeanbrun, no year of tolerance above the micro ceilings, IS company assumed to meet the PME conditions.

## 11. Points to confirm with the user
1. **PS rate on LMNP income: 18.6 % from 2025 income.** LFSS 2026 art. 12 raises CSG to 10.6 % on the L136-6 revenus du patrimoine and keeps 9.2 % only for revenus fonciers (L136-6 I a), real-estate plus-values (L136-7 I 2°), life insurance and home-savings plans. impots.gouv says: "Les revenus fonciers, les plus-values immobilières, l'assurance-vie, l'épargne logement restent soumis au taux de 9,2 %" and "la hausse … s'applique à compter des revenus 2025". LMNP BIC is not on the excluded list, so it moves to 18.6 %; most commentators agree. service-public F2329 shows no resident row for furnished lets, and one law firm reads 2025 investment income as still at 17.2 % (true for the L136-7 withheld-at-source income only). The spec uses 18.6 % for LMNP and micro-BIC and 17.2 % for fonciers and all plus-values. A parliamentary question (AN 17685) challenges the retroactivity, so a later change is possible.
2. **Order of ARD and prior BIC deficits in LMNP réel.** The spec follows the reading of CGI 39 C II (amortization limited first, then deficits). Some tools, and the current code, set deficits against the result first. No BOFiP paragraph found that settles it. Golden cases avoid the question (R > 0 every year).
3. **Which components the deducted amortization belongs to** (needed for the add-back, which excludes furniture): pro rata of the building and furniture pools each year. This is a model convention with no legal source.
4. **Real works in the plus-value of LMNP réel.** Are amortized works (amortization later added back) "not already taken into account" and so eligible for the majoration? The spec says yes. Golden cases avoid the question (the 15 % flat rate wins).
5. **Base of the 7.5 % and 15 % flat rates** for LMNP after the add-back: the spec uses the contractual price.
6. **21,400 € cap**: does the extra 10,700 € cover only the share of the deficit coming from energy works (spec), or the whole deficit once such works exist? Confirmed: the cap applies to payments made up to 31 Dec 2027 (service-public F1991, checked April 2026, extended by LF 2026).
7. **Micro-BIC ceiling for 2026 income: 83,600 €** (service-public F32744, revalued from 77,700 €). Only one official page states it. Confirm before hard-coding.
8. **CFE**: year of creation exempt (CGI 1478 II, not checked online) and the 5,000 € receipts exemption (CGI 1647 D, AN question 7364). Sources differ on the reference period: the current year's receipts or year N−2. The spec uses the current year. The amount varies by commune (the defaults use 150 € and 500 €; which one?).
9. **Surtax for couples**: the threshold applies to each spouse's share, so a couple owning 50/50 pays no surtax below 100 k€ of combined gain. The spec assumes one cédant. Do we add an `owners` parameter?
10. **Several properties**: is the 39 C II limit applied per property or across the whole furnished activity? The spec applies it per property, then nets results at foyer level.
11. **Regime per foyer, not per property** (§8). This contradicts the DEV_PLAN T23 wording "chosen per property". Please confirm the reinterpretation.
12. **IS rates**: 15 % up to 42,500 € and 25 % above, unchanged by LF 2026 (the PLF amendment raising the threshold to 100 k€ was dropped). Only secondary sources were found; Légifrance was not readable from here.
13. **Dispositif Jeanbrun** (LF 2026, loi 2026-103, CGI 31 I 1° j): a new amortization for unfurnished lets, 80 % of the price, capped rents, 9-year commitment. Sources disagree on the annual cap (10,700 € or 12,000 €) and the decrees may be missing. Out of scope; add it as a regime later?
14. **SCI IS distribution model**: PFU on the boni at exit (default on). Or do you prefer yearly distributions?
15. **Residence-services exception**: the exact list (students, seniors, elderly or disabled people) and its CCH reference (L631-12 according to a law firm) were not verified in the text of CGI 150 VB III, which was not readable here.

## 11b. User decisions (2026-11-13)
- **Points 1–8, 10, 11, 12, 15:** adopted as written in this spec (the user was offered the defaults and raised no objection). Revisit if the law or BOFiP clarifies.
- **Point 8, CFE default:** **300 €/yr per furnished property**, with the year-1 exemption and the exemption below 5,000 € of receipts applied automatically. Unfurnished properties: none.
- **Point 9, co-owners:** add a parameter `nb_owners ∈ {1, 2}` (default 1). The gain is split by owner for the surtax threshold only.
- **Point 13, Jeanbrun:** out of scope for now; tracked as a later task (T68).
- **Point 14, SCI IS:** profits are capitalised. PFU (31.4 %) applies once, on the liquidation boni at exit. No yearly distributions.

## 11c. Implementation readings (T23)
Where the spec left room, T23 (`src/domain/tax/`) implements these readings:
1. **Micro ceilings over the horizon.** The ceiling is checked against the category's highest yearly receipts over the horizon (year 1 or year H, depending on rent indexation). If it is exceeded in any year, the category uses its régime réel for the **whole horizon**, with a warning. This follows "one regime for the whole horizon" (§4.3, §4.4). The model does not switch regime year by year, and the one-year tolerance is still ignored.
2. **Default unfurnished regime: réel.** This mirrors the furnished default (LMNP réel). The legal default is micro-foncier, which the user can pick.
3. **Energy-works cap (§4.5, 11.6).** cap = 10,700 + min(10,700, energy works paid that year), only in simulation years 1–2 (2026–2027). The model assumes that `renovation_energetique` lifts the DPE from E/F/G to A–D.
4. **SCI IS with several properties.** There is one company: the IS brackets apply once to the company's total result, and the professional gains and losses at exit are **netted across properties** (no per-property floor, unlike §1.10 for individuals). CFE still applies to furnished properties held by the SCI. The SCI always amortizes furniture; the `amort_mobilier` switch only affects LMNP réel.
5. **Déficit-foncier recapture with several properties.** Imputations are tracked at foyer level. Every property is sold at H, so the recapture covers all imputations of years N > H − 3.
6. **Vacancy (reworked in T26a).** Vacancy reduces the gross receipts (rent x (1 - vacancy)); it is no longer a deductible charge, so micro allowances apply to the receipts net of vacancy. Year-1 works months also reduce receipts. New operating charges: maintenance/capex provision (EUR/m2/yr, deductible in réel, inside the allowance in micro) and GLI / unpaid-rent premium (% of collected rent, same treatment). Taxe foncière grows at its own rate (default 3 %).
