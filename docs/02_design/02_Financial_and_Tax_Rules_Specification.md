## 1. Executive Summary
This document defines the strict mathematical formulas, loan amortization algorithms, cashflow constraints, and French tax regimes (LMNP, Micro-BIC) that the App Immo Simulation Engine mandates. This acts as the single source of truth for the `domain/calculator/` and `core/glossary.py` modules. Any code executing financial math must strictly adhere to the constraints defined in this document to avoid compounding statistical errors during 25-year simulation loops.

## 2. Core Financial Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

### 2.1 Debt Service Coverage Ratio (DSCR)
The system evaluates portfolio risk using DSCR, computed post-simulation from Year 1 data in `glossary.py:calculate_dscr_metric()`.
* **Formula:** `DSCR = NOI / Total Debt Service (Annual)`
  * `NOI = Loyers Bruts + Charges Déductibles (sign-negative in DF) + Intérêts & Assurance`  (reconstructed from yearly DataFrame row)
  * `Total Debt Service = Capital Remboursé (Y1) + Intérêts & Assurance (Y1)`
* **Constraint Limits:**
  * If Total Debt Service < `1e-9`, DSCR returns a cap of `10.0` (infinite DSCR).
  * **Important:** The `PortfolioAllocator` does NOT target DSCR. It targets **cash flow** (`target_cf ± tolerance`). DSCR is computed solely as a scoring metric (weight 0.15 in fitness function) after simulation, not used to gate allocation decisions.

### 2.2 Internal Rate of Return (IRR)
The mathematical metric for comparing the absolute profitability of portfolios, factoring in the time value of money over the entire holding period (e.g., 25 years), inclusive of exit sales.
* **Formula:** Derived via `numpy_financial.irr()`. 
* **Cash Flow Array Construction (`[-C_0, C_1, C_2 ... C_n + Terminal_Value]`):**
    * `Year 0`: `-Total Capital Invested (Apport + Initial Notary/Agency Fees not covered by loan)`.
    * `Years 1 to N-1`: `Net Annual Cash Flow (Rent - Taxes - PMT - Maintenance)`.
    * `Year N`: `Net Annual Cash Flow + (Net Resale Value - Remaining Loan Principal)`.
* **Constraint Limits:** If `irr()` computation yields `NaN` due to all positive flows or missing initial capital outlay, the system defaults the IRR rating to `0.0` or flags as an undefined edge case.

### 2.3 Net Enrichment (Enrichissement Net)
An absolute valuation of wealth generated by the portfolio at year N.
* **Formula (as implemented):**
  * `Liquidation Nette = Valeur Biens (Y_N) - Dette Finale - Frais de Vente - Impôt Plus-Value - IRA`
  * `Enrichissement Net = Liquidation Nette - Apport Initial`
  * Note: Cumulative cash flows are NOT separately summed and subtracted. They appear implicitly in the IRR `flux` array (each year's `cash_flow_net` is appended). The enrichment formula is a one-shot exit-value calculation, not a running CF accumulator.

## 3. Loan & Amortization Rules

### 3.1 Standard Credit Calculation (PMT)
* **Library Native Approach:** Must utilize `numpy_financial.pmt(rate, nper, pv, fv=0, when='end')`.
* **Variables:**
  * `rate`: Annual interest rate / 12 (Monthly), exclusive of insurance.
  * `nper`: Loan duration in years * 12 (Months).
  * `pv`: Total borrowed amount (Target Acquisition Cost - Apport).
* **Constraints:** Must return an absolute scalar representing the monthly debt service. Must mathematically handle edge cases where `pv <= 0` by directly returning `0.0` to bypass calculation crashes.

### 3.2 Amortization Tables
The `financial.py` domain service must generate standard French constant-payment amortization tables.
* **Interest Output:** `Previous Balance * Monthly Rate`.
* **Principal Amortized Output:** `Monthly PMT - Interest Output`.
* **Remaining Balance Output:** `Previous Balance - Principal Amortized Output`.
* **Performance Rule:** The core amortization mapping must employ `functools.lru_cache` for speed, ensuring identical loan structures generated by the optimizer are instantly fetched from memory, not recalculated.

## 4. French Tax Regimes

The simulation engine fundamentally pivots on calculating localized tax laws affecting the Net Operating Income over 25 years. 

### 4.1 LMNP (Loueur en Meublé Non Professionnel) - Regime Réel
This is the default highly optimized tax wrapper calculated by the engine. It models comprehensive depreciation (amortissement) rules to offset rental income.
* **Taxable Income Rule:** `Rent - Deductible Charges (Interest, Insurance, Property Taxes, Maintenance) - Annual Depreciation Allowance`.
* **Depreciation Mechanics (as implemented in `simulation.py`):**
  * **Building:** `base_amortissable = (prix_achat_bien - valeur_terrain) + budget_travaux + renovation_energetique`. Linear depreciation over `amort_immo_years` (default: **30 years**, i.e., ~3.33%/year). Land (`valeur_terrain`) is excluded per French tax law. Land exclusion is controlled by the `ratio_terrain_pct` parameter. If explicitly configured, it strictly enforces that percentage. If not explicitly set (`None`), the ratio defaults dynamically using a sliding scale based on the property's geographic tension (`tension_locative_score_norm`: 0.0 to 1.0), representing a realistic land ratio between **15%** (rural/low tension) and **25%** (prime/high tension).
  * **Furniture (Mobilier):** Linear depreciation of `mobilier / amort_mobilier_years` (default: **10 years**, i.e., 10%/year). Enabled only when `TaxParams.amort_mobilier = True` (default: True).
* **Deficit Carryforward Constraint:** If `NOI (Rents - Deductible Charges) < 0`, the deficit is securely logged into a chronological array (`deficits_reportables`). Amortizations are deferred into `ard_reportable` (Amortissements Reportés Différés). Deficits are absorbed oldest-first in subsequent profitable years. **A strict 10-year expiration limit is mathematically enforced**: any LMNP deficit vintage failing to be absorbed within 10 years automatically expires and falls out of the array, mirroring precise French tax constraints.
* **ARD (Amortissements Reportés Différés) Logic:** In LMNP, amortizations cannot create a deficit. If amortizations exceed positive NOI, the surplus becomes ARD carried forward without expiry.
* **Effective Tax Rate Assignment:** Tax on remaining positive income equates to: `User Marginal Tax Bracket (TMI) + Social Contributions (Prélèvements Sociaux: 17.2%)`.

### 4.2 Micro-BIC (Forfaitaire)
A simplified, unoptimized fallback tax scenario occasionally utilized as a comparative heuristic.
* **Taxable Income Rule:** `Total Rent * (1.0 - micro_bic_abatt_pct / 100.0)`. Default abatement: **50%** (standard furnished rentals). Extended abatement of **71%** available for classé/touristic properties (configurable via `TaxParams.micro_bic_abatt_pct`).
* **Effective Tax Rate:** `Taxable Income × (TMI% + 17.2%)` — identical structure to LMNP tax application.

### 4.3 SCI IS (Société Civile Immobilière à l'Impôt sur les Sociétés)
A corporate tax wrapper frequently selected via the UI, newly active in the branch simulation logic.
* **Taxable Income Rule:** `NOI = Rent - Deductible Charges (Interest, Insurance, Property Taxes, Maintenance) - Annual Depreciation Allowance`. Unlike LMNP, amortizations *can* produce a structural deficit.
* **Deficit Carryforward Constraint:** The deficit accumulates sequentially in the tracking array `deficits_reportables`. Because corporate deficits in France can be perpetually carried forward, there is **no 10-year array truncation** governing IS deficits. They carry forward indefinitely until mathematically absorbed.
* **Effective Tax Rate Assignment:** Corporate tax thresholds are strictly adhered to:
  * **15%** flat tax precisely up to **42,500€** base_imposable.
  * **25%** marginal tax on everything exceeding 42,500€.
  * Social Contributions (17.2%) are fully waived from corporate net income (implicitly zeroed out).
* **Capital Gains Exit Taxation:** The calculation of Net Resale Value switches context dramatically at liquidation: VNC (Valeur Nette Comptable / Net Book Value) acts as the baseline reference `VNC = Purchase Price + Fees + Works - Accumulated Depreciation`. The `Plus-value` becomes subject entirely to IS bracket ceilings rather than individual PV rates, minus any remaining `deficits_reportables` inventory.

### 4.4 Exit Taxation (Plus-Value Immobilière)
Modeled rigorously at Year N when extracting terminal IRR values.
* **Base Calculation:** `Plus-Value = Sale Price - Prix Corrigé`
  * `Prix Corrigé = prix_achat_bien + frais_notaire + budget_travaux + renovation_energetique`
  * In LMNP scope: previously taken depreciation allowances do **not** reduce the acquisition cost basis (unlike LMP). ✅ Correctly implemented.
* **Duration Abatements (exact rates as implemented):**
  * **Years 1–5:** No abatement. Flat rate: 19% IR + 17.2% PS on full plus-value.
  * **Years 6–21:** IR abatement = `6% × (year - 5)` per year (max 66% at Year 21). PS abatement = `1.65% × (year - 5)` per year (max 26.4% at Year 21).
  * **Year 22:** IR abatement = **100%** (full IR exemption). PS abatement += 1.60% additional step.
  * **Years 23–30:** PS abatement += `9% × (year - 22)` per year.
  * **Year 30+:** PS abatement = **100%** (full social charges exemption).
  * Implemented in `simulation.py:_calculate_capital_gains_tax()`.

## 5. Economic & Property Overlays (Macro assumptions)

* **Rent Inflation Constraint:** Calculated geometrically year-over-year. E.g., `Year_2_Rent = Year_1_Rent * (1.0 + Annual_Inflation_Rate)`.
* **Capital Appreciation Constraint:** Modifies `valeur_biens` year-over-year geometrically from Year 2 onward: `valeur_biens *= (1 + appreciation_bien_pct / 100)`. Note: appreciation applies to `prix_achat_bien` aggregate, **not** to `cout_total` (which includes notary fees and renovation). This means renovation and fees do not benefit from price appreciation in the simulation.
* **Notary Fees (Frais de Notaire):** Applied statically at Year 0 as `prix_achat * frais_notaire_pct / 100`. Default: **7.5%** (as set in `FinancingConfig.frais_notaire_pct`). The relevant field is user-configurable in the UI sidebar credit tab.
